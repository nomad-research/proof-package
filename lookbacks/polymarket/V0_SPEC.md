# V0: market-structure audit (descriptive, no gate)

Purpose: say plainly what Polymarket offers a hedge builder, apart from whether the session's hedges were any good. V0 has no pass or fail and does not touch V1's verdict.

Questions:
1. **What is open right now?** Domain and structure mix of the open, usable events in the census (12,353 events).
2. **What did the menu contain in V1?** Distinct events across the 24 menus, and how often each appears.
3. **Supply versus demand.** The asset classes the sessions asked for (from their search terms) against what the menu held for that round, and what the session did about it (hedged on a proxy, hedged structurally, or declined).
4. **Can it be traded?** Volume of the events the sessions actually hedged with against the menu.

Method limits, stated up front: the domain taxonomy is a keyword list I wrote after reading the 179 menu titles, so it is tuned to this sample and is a transparent heuristic, not a measurement instrument. Term-to-domain mapping uses the same list. Nothing here uses prices, outcomes or scores. Order books, spreads and fills are not in this container (the recorder runs on Rob's machine), so depth and cost are not audited here; that needs recorder data and is listed as a follow-up.
