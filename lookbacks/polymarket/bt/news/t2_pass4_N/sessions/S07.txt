If no JMA advisory plots the center on land but credible reporting confirms a crossing occurred between discrete advisory fixes, the market still resolves, with intensity taken from the most recently issued JMA advisory prior to the confirmed crossing time. A disagreement exists only when a JMA advisory and credible reporting make contradictory claims about the storm's position at the same point in time; in that case, JMA is the default resolution source.   Write "low": for each chance 10, 25, 50, 75 and 90, the level you give that chance of being reached (at or below) within the period. The 10 level is the lowest.
