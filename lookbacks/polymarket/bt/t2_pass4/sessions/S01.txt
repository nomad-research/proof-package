  Write "high": for each chance 10, 25, 50, 75 and 90, the level you give that chance of being reached (at or above) within the period. The 10 level is the highest.
  Write "low": for each chance 10, 25, 50, 75 and 90, the level you give that chance of being reached (at or below) within the period. The 10 level is the lowest.
