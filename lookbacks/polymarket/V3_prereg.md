# V3: forward test (pre-registration, written before the crawl, pool or draw)

**Question.** Same as V1, but on events whose outcomes nobody knows yet: does a thesis-first hedge, authored by an independent session from the same prompts, raise the exact-payoff floor and realise a better result than plain de-risking, with the session's causal claims holding? Because the outcomes are in the future, the model's training cutoff no longer matters and the recall canary is kept only as a record.

**What is reused unchanged.** `v1_packet.py` (prompts, validation, tier mapping), `v1_run.py` (prompt files, transcript ingest, audit, reserve and replacement), `v1_score.py` (exact-payoff solver, fees, arms P, C, D, R, M, and the B1 to B4 bar). Nothing in those files is edited; `v3_run.py` wraps them the way `v1_variance.py` does, pointing them at `v3/` and at the open-event index.

**Differences from V1 (all of them).**
1. **Lock.** One lock for every round: the Unix time written by `v3_pool.py --crawl` (`v3/v3_lock.json`), written once. Every packet says `as_of` that date. Prices are read only as eligibility (a price point within the 48 hours before the lock), as in V1.
2. **Pool.** Events open at the lock, in the same classes (exact partition, open partition, ladder, date ladder), same scope tags, same usability rule (event volume at least $100k and at least 3 markets at $10k), and a **horizon** H = the latest scheduled market `endDate` within the event, between 14 and 75 days after the lock.
3. **Menu.** Every open usable exact-structure event at the lock (same rules as V1's menu: at most 80, at least 2 eligible contracts each, at most 12 shown, seeded shuffle) **restricted to contracts whose scheduled `endDate` is on or before the primary's H**, so every leg can resolve inside the window and the payoff stays exact. This is a stricter menu than V1's and will be thinner; that is intended and reported.
4. **Draw.** 30 events, seed 20261001: at least 4 partitions, at most 8 crypto; reserve 10 (at most 3 crypto), used strictly in listed order under the same replacement rules as V1 (A14 and A15).
5. **Scoring time.** A round is scored when every contract it uses (primary basket, hedges, tier-mapped rungs) has resolved. A round with any unresolved contract 45 days after its H is **unscored, reason "unresolved"**, counted in the tally, and a failed round is never dropped for a bad result. Resolution by UMA dispute: handled as V1 (labelled, sensitivity only).
6. **Answers are frozen by hash, with a timestamp, before any outcome exists.** All 30 rounds are authored, then `v3_answers_manifest.json` is written and committed; the commit time is the evidence that no outcome was known. Scoring starts only after.

**Bar (same as V1, stated again so it cannot drift).** Gates: at least 20 scored rounds and at least 10 of them where the primary lost. B1: realised outcome inside the declared reachable set in at least 90% of scored rounds. B2: mean C minus D positive with a 90% bootstrap interval above zero. B3: M (the hedge from the next round) shows no effect, and C beats the random hedge R in at least 65% of rounds. B4: retention at least 0.5 when the primary won. Verdict: inconclusive below the gates; dead if B1 fails or B2's mean is not positive; pass only if all hold. No result is a statement about profit.

**Declared limits.** Hedge events have only one resolution each: for a ladder, resolution at H means "by date" rungs resolve at their dates. Same-underlying (structural) hedges are flagged with the V1_STRUCTURAL.md rule and reported as a separate row, not removed. The menu needs a priced contract on each leg at the lock, not depth: order books are not audited here (V0 follow-up). Forward prices may be checked later against the recorder's universe files for the same date as an independent record of the lock state.

**Cost.** About 85k tokens a round, so about 2.6M for 30 rounds plus replacements; the variance check is not repeated.

Same crawl, lock, horizon window (14 to 150 days), seed, draw rules, reserve size (20) and bar.
